What We Offer

Our Services

From weekly upkeep to seasonal transformations, we cover every part of keeping a garden healthy and beautiful.

Regular Maintenance:

  • Lawn mowing and grass cutting
  • Edge trimming along walkways and walls
  • Weed control in beds and paths
  • Blowing or sweeping hard surfaces

Plant Care:

  • Pruning and shaping shrubs and bushes
  • Hedge cutting and fence line trimming
  • Deadheading flowers and removing dead leaves
  • Seasonal planting of seedlings or flowers

Clean-Up & Extras

  • Garden refuse bagging and removal
  • Leaf clearing during autumn or seasonal shifts
  • Irrigation checks or basic system monitoring
  • Lawn dressing and fertilizing
Good To Know

How We Price & Schedule

Every garden is different, so we don't use one-size-fits-all pricing. After a free site visit, we put together a written quote based on your garden's size, condition and how often you'd like us to visit — weekly, fortnightly, monthly, or as a once-off. There's no obligation to continue after the first visit.
